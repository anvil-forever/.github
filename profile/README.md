# Anvil Forever

**Small tools that fix one real annoyance. Free, no ads, nothing locked.**

Add-ons for WoW Forever, built in the open under GPLv3.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | What it fixes | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Anvil Escort Guard](https://github.com/anvil-forever/AnvilEscortGuard) | Quest add-ons (Questie, RXP and others) auto-accepting escort quests before your group is ready. You still click Accept yourself. | In testing, not released yet |

## Found a bug?

1. In game, type `/aeg bug`. A short report opens, already selected. Copy it.
2. Paste it into a new issue on the add-on's repository, e.g. [Escort Guard issues](https://github.com/anvil-forever/AnvilEscortGuard/issues).

One bug per issue, please. We reply on the issue, and say which version fixes it.

## How we're built

Anvil Forever is one player working with a team of AI agents. The agents write and test most of the code; a person decides what we build, tests every full release in game, and approves everything we post.

## Free, and staying free

No paid features, no early access, no ads, and never a link or an ask inside the game.
